RLQuantOpt

Fast, robust perfect-entangling gates via reinforcement learning

RL agents shape the microwave control pulses of two coupled transmon qubits, reaching a perfect-entangling gate at the quantum speed limit of the model. v1 is the paper in Quantum Science and Technology (2026); v2 re-implements everything in JAX and moves toward what hardware demands: a policy that reads only a routine frequency calibration and plays a whole pulse open loop on a drifted device.

~10 nsentangling gate at the speed limit
~100×faster simulation in JAX v2
~40kenv steps/s on a laptop CPU

RL for tokamak current ramp-up

ITER hybrid-scenario ramp-up on the Gym-TORAX benchmark

Every second for 150 s, the agent sets the plasma current and the NBI and ECRH heating power. The repo reproduces the benchmark paper's PI and open-loop baselines on its exact software stack, then adds PPO, SAC, MBPO, behaviour cloning, TD3+BC and MOPO, with every checkpoint committed. Along the way it found that the benchmark reward can be gamed, and proposes an audited score.

3.79PI baseline, reproduced exactly
3.95MBPO after 1,364 simulator steps
150 sramp-up, 3 actuators, 60-d state

RL for rocket engine control

Deep RL for a liquid-propellant engine, anchored on the LUMEN Control Challenge

LUMEN is DLR's 25 kN liquid-oxygen / methane expander-bleed engine. Two turbine valves must track chamber pressure (thrust) and mixture ratio through model error, drift and faults. The site reviews the field from 2018 to 2026, sets published designs side by side and ranks open questions. The baselines are ready to build as soon as the challenge simulator is released.

25 kNLOX / methane demonstrator
1.3 %DLR's SAC tracking error on the real engine
7benchmark cases, nominal to faults

RL for Flatland train rescheduling

Can multi-agent RL close the gap to operations research on railways?

Trains on a grid-drawn network must reach their targets inside timetable windows without sharing cells, while random malfunctions stop them. Operations research has won every official Flatland round. This verified review (2019–2026) comes with four fixed scenarios, an OR reference that reproduces the core of the winning planners, and PPO and imitation-then-PPO baselines, all scored by one harness.

95.7 %OR planner, 100 trains on 100×100
16.0 %best RL policy on the same scenario
< 1 htrain + evaluate, laptop CPU
